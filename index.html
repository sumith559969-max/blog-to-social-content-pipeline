Blog → Social Content Pipeline

Make.com Automation Project

One article.
Three social platforms.

An automated workflow that transforms RSS articles into platform-specific social media drafts using AI and stores them in Airtable for review.

How It Works

RSS Feed → Jina AI → Google Gemini → Airtable

Platforms Supported

LinkedIn

Generates concise professional social media drafts.

X

Generates short posts with the original article URL.

Instagram

Generates concise captions based on article content.

Technology Stack

Make.com · Airtable · Jina AI · Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite

Project Status

The RSS-to-Airtable draft-generation workflow has been tested successfully. Human approval-gated publishing, automatic scheduling, and advanced error handling remain future improvements.
